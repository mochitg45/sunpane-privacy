Privacy Policy — Sunpane: Stained Glass Puzzle

Effective 28 September 2026. Applies to the Android app com.inatasunsoft.sunpane, published by Inata Sun Soft.

Sunpane is a single-player puzzle game. There is no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and the game does not send your progress anywhere. This policy explains the small amount of data that the third-party services inside the app handle.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, hints and settings) is saved in your device's private app storage and stays there. We run no server and keep no database of players. Uninstalling the app deletes your progress.

What third-party services collect

ServiceWhy it is in the appWhat it handles
Google AdMobRewarded video ads (watched only when you ask for a free hint) and full-screen ads between levels, which start after level 20.Advertising ID, device and app identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction events. Used to select and measure ads.
RevenueCat, with Google Play BillingRuns the one-time Remove Ads purchase and restores it if you reinstall.A pseudonymous app user ID generated on your device, purchase history, country and device platform. It never receives your payment card; Google Play takes the payment.

Their own policies: Google policies.google.com/privacy (and how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites); RevenueCat revenuecat.com/privacy.

Advertising choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before the first ad request, and you can reopen it from the "Privacy options" link on the title screen. Buying Remove Ads turns off the ads between levels permanently; rewarded ads stay available, but only when you choose to watch one for a hint.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of the services above, contact us and we will help you raise it with that provider.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. To reach the data the services above hold:

  • AdMob: reset your Advertising ID, or use Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
  • RevenueCat: write to us with your Google Play order ID and we will request deletion of the associated records on your behalf.
